# 🎲 DiceDrop

[Screenshots](#screenshots) | [License](#license)

**DiceDrop** is a clean and thoughtful puzzle game where every move is decided by the roll of the dice.

Simple rules, growing tension, and one constant question: will luck be on your side this turn?

### 🧩 Gameplay

Every turn starts with a roll of two dice, shaping the next block you must place on the board.

Each roll is unpredictable. You can trust your luck and hope the next block will fit — or carefully manage the field to stay ready for any outcome.  
A single bad roll on an unprepared board is enough to end the game.

### 🛠️ Bonuses

When chance turns against you, bonuses offer a way to regain control:

- 🔄 **Rotate** — adjust the block to fit tight spaces  
- 🎲 **Reroll** — take another chance and change the outcome  
- 💣 **Bomb** — clears a cross-shaped area across the board  
- 🔨 **Hammer** — removes blocks in a 3×3 zone  

Used at the right moment, they can mean the difference between survival and defeat.

### 🛍️ Shop

The shop supports different playstyles:

- 🎁 Daily rewards for steady progress  
- 🛒 Guaranteed bonuses for careful planners  
- 🎲 Random bonuses for those who prefer to test their luck  

### 🏆 Achievements

Achievements track your journey through chance and repetition.

They reward persistence, risk-taking, and long-term commitment — from your first rolls to hundreds of throws later.

### 🎨 Visual Style

- Clean, focused visuals  
- Smooth animations and clear feedback  
- Designed to keep attention on the board and the next roll  

### ✨ Final Notes

**DiceDrop** is about living with uncertainty.

You can rely on luck — or prepare for the worst.  
Either way, the dice will eventually decide.
